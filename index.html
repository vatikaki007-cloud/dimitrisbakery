About Dimitri's Bakery

What Makes Us Stand Out?

Dimitri's Bakery is a family owned and run business.

Founded by the late Dimitri and now run by Anna, her son, Dimitri and aided by Anna's mom. Dimitri's Bakery started out just doing rolls, then branched into pastries.

Now Dimitri's is a full turnkey confectioner and bakery – doing everything from cakes to full scale catering.
